# Sources and claims

After authenticating the receipt-selected `{planFile, planHash}`, every claim-bearing drafting task
must use the deterministic section index to name its exact outline and draft deliverables, mapped
`CLAIM-NN` IDs, and **Source Plan** context. A task may not tell a drafter to find, infer, or invent
sources while drafting. Any unresolved source acquisition or claim support belongs before
implementation.

Reviewers verify each proposition and citation against the PLAN Claims, Claim → Section Map,
Source Plan, and current PLAN-bound section deliverables. A visible précis, master outline,
workflow state, or review ledger cannot supply source or claim authority.
