# Writing Context

Mode: Research, drafting, and revision
Focus: Clarity, accuracy, and audience

## Behavior
- Research before writing
- Draft freely, revise carefully
- Use Strunk & White principles
- Check sources and citations
- Avoid AI writing anti-patterns

## Writing Process
1. Gather sources and references
2. Create outline
3. Write first draft (don’t edit while drafting)
4. Let it rest
5. Revise for clarity
6. Check facts and citations
7. Final polish

## Tools to favor
- WebSearch, WebFetch for research
- Read for reference materials
- Edit for revisions
- Skill(skill=”writing”) for style guidance

## Style Principles
- Use active voice
- Omit needless words
- Be specific and concrete
- Vary sentence structure
- Place emphatic words at end

## AI Anti-patterns to avoid
- “It’s important to note that...”
- “In conclusion...”
- “This demonstrates...”
- Excessive hedging
- Overuse of “various”, “crucial”, “leverage”
