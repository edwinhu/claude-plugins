# Statutory and Regulatory Citations - Bluebook Rules 12-14

Guidance for citing statutes, regulations, and legislative materials per Bluebook **22nd Edition**.

**Verification status:** Rule 12 below was checked line by line against the official 22e text of
rules 12.1-12.10 (22e pp. 126-40). Rules 11, 13, 14, and 21 were **not** available for
verification; every statement under those headings is flagged UNVERIFIED in place.

## Federal Statutes (Rule 12)

### Basic Form

```
[Title] U.S.C. § [Section].
```

**Example:**
```
42 U.S.C. § 1983.
```

**No date is required for federal code citations.** Rule 12.3.2 (22e pp. 128-31): "Citations to
the federal code, whether official or unofficial, do not require a date." A year in parentheses is
neither wrong nor required for U.S.C.; it is required only for state code volumes, and even then
only per rule 12.3.2's spine/title-page/copyright preference order.

Code names are **printed in small capitals** (rule 12.3, 22e pp. 128-31): "All citations to codes
should contain the abbreviated name of the code found in table T1 printed in small capitals; the
section, paragraph, or article number(s) of the statute; and the year of the code."

### United States Code (U.S.C.)

**Preferred, but not required.** Rule 12.1 (22e pp. 126-27): "Citing official codes is preferable,
but not required." Rule 12.2.1(a) states the order of preference for statutes currently in force:
current official code or its supplement; then current unofficial code or its supplement; then
official session laws; then privately published session laws; then a commercial electronic
database; then a looseleaf service; then an internet source; then a newspaper.

**Unofficial federal codes:**
- U.S.C.A. (West)
- U.S.C.S. (LexisNexis)

Federal statutes enacted after the latest U.S.C. edition or supplement are cited to an unofficial
code until they appear in the U.S.C. (rule 12.2.1(a)). PDF copies of the U.S.C. at
govinfo.gov "may be cited as if they were the print code."

**Year:** For a *state* code volume, cite the year of the code edition, not the year of enactment
(rule 12.3.2).

```
Correct:   Neb. Rev. Stat. § 33-114 (1998).    ← year on spine/title page
Wrong:     Neb. Rev. Stat. § 33-114 (1871).    ← enactment year
```

### Multiple Sections

**Consecutive sections:**
```
15 U.S.C. §§ 13–13b.
```

**Non-consecutive sections:**
```
42 U.S.C. §§ 1983, 1985, 1988.
```

Note the 22e prints spans with an en dash (`§§ 2421–2424`, `83 Stat. 852, 853–54`), not a hyphen.

### Subsections

```
42 U.S.C. § 1983(a).
42 U.S.C. § 1983(a)(1).
42 U.S.C. § 1983(a)(1)(A).
```

### Supplements

Cite supplements and pocket parts per rule 12.3.1(e), which routes to rule 3.1(c):
```
18 U.S.C. § 510(b) (Supp. I 1983).
12 U.S.C. § 1455 (1982 & Supp. I 1983).
```

For a state code, give the year on the supplement's title page, or its latest copyright year
(rule 12.3.2):
```
Ind. Code Ann. § 29-1-5-3.1 (West Supp. 2003).
Vt. Stat. Ann. tit. 12, § 892 (2002 & Supp. 2004).
```

### Names and Original Section Numbers

Rule 12.3.1(a) (22e pp. 128-31): "Give the statute's name and original section number (as it
appears in the appropriate session laws) **only if** the statute is commonly cited that way or if
the information would otherwise aid in identification. **Omit 'The' as the first word of a
statute's name.** Include the year of the statute if it is in the official title. An official
name, a popular name, or both may be used."

The name goes **before** the code cite, not in a trailing parenthetical:
```
Labor Management Relations (Taft-Hartley) Act § 301(a), 29 U.S.C. § 185(a).
Family Medical Leave Act (FMLA) of 1993, 29 U.S.C. § 2601.
National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 § 102, 42 U.S.C. § 4332.
```

### Compilations, Appendices, and Notes

- **Uncodified laws in a separate compilation** (rule 12.3.1(f)): `N.Y. Unconsol. Law § 751
  (McKinney 2000).`
- **Appendices** (rule 12.3.1(g)): if the statute is numbered and printed as if part of the code,
  cite per rule 3.4 — `50 U.S.C. app. § 5.` If not, cite the session laws and add `reprinted in`
  — `Act of Aug. 31, 1970, ch. 842, 1970 Mass. Acts 732, reprinted in Mass. Gen. Laws Ann. ch. 40
  app. at 180 (West 1985).`
- **Notes** (rule 12.3.1(h)): `12 U.S.C. § 1821 note (Continuation of Health Plan Coverage in
  Cases of Failed Financial Institutions).`

## Session Laws (Rule 12.4)

### When to Use

- Statutes appearing in so many scattered sections that no useful code citation is possible
  (rule 12.2.2(a))
- The historical fact of enactment, amendment, or repeal (rule 12.2.2(b))
- Language in the current code differs materially from the session laws and the title has not been
  enacted into positive law (rule 12.2.2(c))
- Private laws (rule 12.2.1(c)) — `Priv. L. No. 94-75, 90 Stat. 2985 (1976).`

### Public Laws

```
National Environmental Policy Act of 1969, Pub. L. No. 91-190, § 102, 83 Stat. 852, 853–54 (1970).
```

Format:
```
[Name], Pub. L. No. [Congress]-[Law No.], § [Section], [Vol.] Stat. [First Page], [Pinpoint Page(s)] ([Year]).
```

**Pages (rule 12.4(c)):** citing an entire act, give only the page where the act begins; citing
part of an act, give the section(s), the page where the act begins, **and** the page(s) where the
cited sections appear.

**Name (rule 12.4(a)):** "always give the name of the statute and the public law or chapter
number." Omit "The" as the first word. If the statute has no official or popular name, use
"Act of <date of enactment>" or, if unavailable, "Act effective <date of effectiveness>":
```
Act of Aug. 21, 1974, ch. 85, 1974 N.J. Laws 385 (providing unemployment compensation for jurors).
```

**Year (rule 12.4(e)):** give the year the legislature passed the statute; if no enactment date is
identified, give the effective date. **Omit the year if the same year already appears in the name
of the statute or of the session laws:**
```
Securities Act of 1933, ch. 38, 48 Stat. 74 (codified as amended at 15 U.S.C. §§ 77a–77aa).
```

**Amending acts (rule 12.4(d)):** cite the bill's own sections with the abbreviation `sec.` and
the amended act's sections with the symbol `§`:
```
Labor-Management Relations Act, ch. 120, sec. 101, § 8(a)(3), 61 Stat. 136, 140–41 (1947).
```

**Codification information (rule 12.4(f)) — changed in the 22e.** Give the code location
parenthetically only "if a statute has been or will ultimately be codified, the code location is
known, **and the codification is relevant in the context of the citation**." The 22e adds: "Note
that this rule is context dependent and permits codification information to be **omitted** if the
statute is not being cited for its present application."
```
Act of July 12, 1985, ch. 223, § 3, 1985 Cal. Legis. Serv. 239, 241 (West) (to be codified at Cal. Ins. Code § 11589.5).
```

### Statutes at Large

```
Act of July 2, 1890, ch. 647, 26 Stat. 209.
```

**Volume (rule 12.4(b)):** give the volume number (or, if none, the year), then the session-law
name in **ordinary roman type**. State session laws begin with the state abbreviated per table T10
even when the state name is not part of the official title; abbreviations are in tables T1.3 and
T1.4:
```
1878 Minn. Laws.
Not: 1878 Laws of Minn.
```

## State Statutes (Rule 12.3)

### Basic Form

```
[State Code Abbrev.] [Topic] § [Section] ([Publisher] [Year]).
```

Rule 12.3 (22e pp. 128-31): "As with federal statutes, citing the official state codes is
preferred, but not required."

### Examples by Format

**Subject-matter codes (rule 12.3.1(c)):**
```
Cal. Veh. Code § 11506 (West 2000).
Tex. Fam. Code Ann. § 5.01 (Vernon 2002 & Supp. 2004–2005).
```

**Title/chapter codes (rule 12.3.1(b)):**
```
Del. Code Ann. tit. 13, § 1301 (1999).
Nev. Rev. Stat. § 28.501 (1998).
```

If each title, chapter, or volume of a code has differently numbered sections, the title number
need not be given separately: `Ga. Code Ann. § 21-2-16 (2003).`

**Looseleaf codes (rule 12.3.2):** give the year on the page where the provision is printed, or on
the first page of its subdivision — `Alaska Stat. § 28.01.010 (2002).`

state codes, and indicates whether a state uses title/chapter numbering or subject-matter naming
(rules 12.3.1(b)-(c)). *T1.3 changed in the 22e; consult the current table rather than any
example cached here.*

### Publisher

Rule 12.3.1(d): give the publisher, editor, or compiler in the year parenthetical "**unless** a
code is published, edited, compiled by, or under the supervision of federal or state officials."
Give the publisher's name, not the jurisdiction's:
```
Correct:   Cal. Veh. Code § 11509 (West 2000).
Wrong:     Cal. Veh. Code § 11509 (Cal. 2000).
```

## Electronic Databases and Online Sources (Rule 12.5)

**Commercial databases (rule 12.5(a), 22e pp. 132-33):** give the database name and the currency
information *the database itself supplies*, **in place of** the year of the code under rule 12.3.2.
Still give the publisher per rule 12.3.1(d):
```
18 U.S.C.S. § 1956 (LexisNexis, LEXIS through Pub. L. No. 113-108).
18 U.S.C.A. § 1956 (West, Westlaw through Pub. L. No. 113-93 (excluding Pub. L. No. 113-79)).
Wis. Stat. Ann. § 19.43 (West, Westlaw through 1995 Act 26).
```

**Internet sources (rule 12.5(b)):** official statutes and ordinances published online may be cited
directly. Authentic, official, or exact online copies are cited as if they were the print source
(rule 18.2.1(a)); unofficial online sources follow rule 18.2.2:
```
Haw. Rev. Stat. § 142-23.5 (2023), https://www.capitol.hawaii.gov/... (on file with the Yale Law Journal).
```

## Other Secondary Sources (Rule 12.6)

When citing a statute to anything other than a code, session laws, or an electronic database, give
the name and public law or chapter number as if citing session laws (rule 12.6, 22e pp. 133-34).
Prefer U.S.C.C.A.N. for federal statutes, especially those enacted after 1974, noting the Statutes
at Large volume (and page, if known):
```
Act of July 19, 1985, Pub. L. No. 99-68, 1985 U.S.C.C.A.N. (99 Stat.) 166.
```

If a recent statute has not yet been published anywhere, give the name, the public law or chapter
number, the section if partial, the full date of enactment (or approval/effective date), and the
future code or session-law location if known:
```
Alabama Corporate Income Tax Reform Act, No. 85-515 (May 8, 1985).
```

## Special Citation Forms (Rule 12.9)

Rule 12.9 (22e pp. 135-39) covers the Internal Revenue Code, ordinances, rules of evidence and
procedure, model codes and restatements, and the ABA materials.

### Internal Revenue Code (Rule 12.9.1)

`26 U.S.C.` may be replaced with `I.R.C.`:
```
26 U.S.C. § 61.   becomes:   I.R.C. § 61.
```
Cited to an unofficial code, put the publisher in the year parenthetical: `I.R.C. § 1371 (West
Supp. 1991).` / `I.R.C. § 1247 (West).`

### Ordinances (Rule 12.9.2)

Always give the political subdivision and abbreviated state name first; do not abbreviate the
subdivision unless table T10 abbreviates it. Codified ordinances: subdivision, state, and code name
in **small capitals**, with the year of the code per rule 12.3.2:
```
Montgomery, Ala., Code § 3A-11 (1971).
S.F., Cal., Police Code art. 16, div. 1, § 1076(a) (2000).
```
Uncodified ordinances: number (or name), exact date of adoption parenthetically, **ordinary roman
type**:
```
S.J., Cal., Ordinance 16,043 (Jan. 17, 1972).
```

### Rules of Evidence and Procedure (Rule 12.9.3)

Small capitals, **without any date**:
```
Fed. R. Civ. P. 12(b)(6).    Fed. R. Evid. 410.    Sup. Ct. R. 17.    1st Cir. R. 6(a).
```
Rules no longer in force get the most recent official source plus the date of repeal:
`Sup. Ct. R. 8, 306 U.S. 690 (1939) (repealed 1954).`

### Model Codes, Restatements, Guidelines, Uniform Acts (Rule 12.9.4)

Small capitals, by section or rule, with **the author's name given parenthetically** alongside the
year (abbreviated per rule 15.1(c)):
```
Restatement (Third) of Unfair Competition § 3 (A.L.I. 1995).
U.S. Sent'g Guidelines Manual § 2D1.1(c) (U.S. Sent'g Comm'n 2021).
U.C.C. § 2-314 (A.L.I. & Unif. L. Comm'n 1977).
Restatement (Second) of Torts § 847A (A.L.I., Tentative Draft No. 17, 1974).
```
For restatements give the year of publication; for model codes, principles, standards, and
guidelines give the year of adoption, and the year of the last amendment even when citing an
unamended portion. Retain a restatement's subtitle: `Restatement (Third) of Prop.: Wills and
Donative Transfers § 2.1 (A.L.I. 1999).` A uniform act cited as the law of a particular state is
cited as that state's code: `Okla. Stat. tit. 12A, § 2-314 (2004).`

### ABA Materials (Rule 12.9.5)

```
Model Rules of Pro. Conduct r. 3.12 (A.B.A., Discussion Draft 1983).
Model Code of Pro. Resp. EC 7-36 (A.B.A. 1980).
A.B.A. Comm. on Ethics & Pro. Resp., Informal Op. 1414 (1978).
```

## Short Forms for Statutes (Rule 12.10)

Rule 12.10(b) (22e pp. 139-40) sets the availability condition: use a short citation only if the
statute "has already been cited (in either full or short form, including 'id.') in either the same
footnote or in a manner such that it can be readily found in **one of the preceding five
footnotes**." Otherwise use the full citation.

### Id.

```
1. 42 U.S.C. § 1983.
2. Id.
3. Id. § 1985.
```

### Section Symbol Alone

Permitted short citation forms per the rule 12.10 table:

| Source | Short citation |
|---|---|
| U.S. Code provisions | `42 U.S.C. § 1983` or `§ 1983` |
| Named statutes | `§ 1` or `5 U.S.C. § 551` or `Administrative Procedure Act § 1` |
| State code (numbered) | `tit. 28, § 1701` or `§ 1701` |
| State code (named) | `Educ. § 48222` |
| Session laws | `§ 102` or `National Environmental Policy Act § 102` or `§ 102, 83 Stat. at 853–54` |

**In text, spell out "section" — except for U.S. Code provisions.** Rule 12.10(c): "except when
referring to United States Code provisions, the word 'section' should be spelled out in law review
text and footnote text, although the symbol '§' may be used in citations."

```
Text (U.S.C.):        42 U.S.C. § 1983 or § 1983
Text (state code):    title 28, section 1701 of the Delaware Code, or section 1701
Text (named act):     section 1 of the Administrative Procedure Act, or section 1
```

### Electronic Sources (Rule 12.10(d))

Build the short form with the database name; a URL need not be repeated after a full citation:
```
Wis. Stat. Ann. § 19.43 (West, Westlaw through 2007 Act 242).   becomes:   § 19.43 (Westlaw).
Utah Code § 4-30-108 (2017), http://le.utah.gov/...              becomes:   § 4-30-108.
```

### Named Acts

```
1. Administrative Procedure Act § 1, 5 U.S.C. § 551.
...
5. Administrative Procedure Act § 1.
```

The short form at footnote 5 works because footnote 1 is inside the **preceding five footnotes**
(12.10(b)). Past that window the full citation returns — an example showing the short form at
footnote 15 with nothing in between would illustrate a violation, not the rule.

## Common Errors

### Section Symbol Errors

| Error | Correction |
|-------|------------|
| §1983 | § 1983 (space required) — UNVERIFIED, rule 6.2 not in corpus |
| Sec. 1983 | § 1983 (in citations) |
| ss. 1983-1985 | §§ 1983–1985 |
| "§ 1701 of the Delaware Code" in text | "section 1701" — spell out in text except for U.S.C. (rule 12.10(c)) |

### Code Errors

| Error | Correction |
|-------|------------|
| 42 USC § 1983 | 42 U.S.C. § 1983 |
| 42 U.S.C. §1983 | 42 U.S.C. § 1983 |
| 42 U.S.C., § 1983 | 42 U.S.C. § 1983 |
| Cal. Veh. Code § 11509 (Cal. 2000) | Cal. Veh. Code § 11509 (West 2000) — publisher, not jurisdiction (rule 12.3.1(d)) |

### Year Errors

| Error | Correction |
|-------|------------|
| Neb. Rev. Stat. § 33-114 (1871) | Neb. Rev. Stat. § 33-114 (1998) ← code year, not enactment |
| Securities Act of 1933, ch. 38, 48 Stat. 74 (1933) | drop the year — it is already in the statute's name (rule 12.4(e)) |
| 42 U.S.C. § 1983 (2018) | not an error, but not required either — federal code citations "do not require a date" (rule 12.3.2) |

## Verification Checklist

Before finalizing any statutory citation:

- [ ] Code name in small capitals (rule 12.3)
- [ ] Official code cited where available — preferred, but not required (rule 12.1)
- [ ] Section symbol (§) used with space before number
- [ ] Double section symbol (§§) for multiple sections; en dash for spans
- [ ] No date on a federal code cite unless you want one (rule 12.3.2)
- [ ] State code year taken from spine, title page, or latest copyright — in that order (rule 12.3.2)
- [ ] Session-law year omitted if already in the statute's or session laws' name (rule 12.4(e))
- [ ] Publisher given for codes not produced by federal or state officials (rule 12.3.1(d))
- [ ] Statute name given only if commonly cited that way or it aids identification; "The" dropped (rules 12.3.1(a), 12.4(a))
- [ ] Codification parenthetical included only if relevant in context (rule 12.4(f))
- [ ] Supplements cited if applicable (rule 12.3.1(e), rule 3.1(c))
- [ ] Short form used only if the source appears in the same footnote or one of the preceding five (rule 12.10(b))
- [ ] "section" spelled out in text except for U.S.C. provisions (rule 12.10(c))

## Special Situations

### Scattered Sections (Rule 12.2.2(a))

Cite the session laws only if the statute appears in so many scattered sections that no useful code
citation is possible, and indicate the general location parenthetically:
```
Tax Reduction Act of 1975, Pub. L. No. 94-12, 89 Stat. 26 (codified as amended in scattered sections of 26 U.S.C.).
```
But where a useful code cite exists, use it: `Robinson-Patman Act, 15 U.S.C. §§ 13–13b, 21a.`

### Invalidated Statutes (Rule 12.7.1)

Cite the invalidating case in full:
```
Religious Freedom Restoration Act (RFRA) of 1993, Pub. L. No. 103-141, 1993 U.S.C.C.A.N. (107 Stat.) 1488, invalidated by, City of Boerne v. Flores, 521 U.S. 507 (1997).
```

### Repealed Statutes (Rule 12.7.2)

Indicate the fact and date of repeal parenthetically, or cite the repealing statute in full when
particularly relevant:
```
Law of June 1, 1895, ch. 4322, § 23, 1895 Fla. Laws ch. 3, 20–21 (repealed 1969).
Act of Jan. 24, 1923, ch. 42, 42 Stat. 1174, 1208, repealed by, Budget and Accounting Procedures Act of 1950, ch. 946, § 301(97), 64 Stat. 832, 844.
```

### Amended Statutes (Rule 12.7.3)

Three options: note the amendment parenthetically, cite the amending statute in full, or give the
current version parenthetically:
```
Supplemental Appropriation Act of 1955, Pub. L. No. 663, § 1311, 68 Stat. 800, 830 (1954) (amended 1959).
33 U.S.C. § 1232 (1982), amended by, 33 U.S.C. § 1232(f) (Supp. I 1983).
Clayton Act, ch. 323, § 7, 38 Stat. 730, 731–32 (1914) (current version at 15 U.S.C. § 18).
```

### Prior History (Rule 12.7.4)

```
33 U.S.C. § 1232(f) (Supp. I 1983) (amending 33 U.S.C. § 1232 (1982)).
28 U.S.C. § 1652 (originally enacted as Act of June 25, 1948, ch. 646, § 1652, 62 Stat. 869, 944).
```

### Explanatory Parentheticals (Rule 12.8)

Beyond code location, dates, and history, a parenthetical may give any other relevant information
about a statute (rule 12.8, 22e p. 135):
```
5 U.S.C. § 553(b) (requiring agencies to publish notice of proposed rulemaking in the Federal Register).
```
